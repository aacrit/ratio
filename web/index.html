What Ratio does

A design consultant that measures the photo and shows the rule. Never the person, only the choice.

Proportion
Where the break between the pieces falls, against the thirds and the golden section.
Volume and line
Fitted against full, and whether the shoes carry the leg line to the floor.
Colour
Harmony, value, shares and chroma, over the outfit's own palette.

Every reading shows what was measured, the rule it was held against, and the advice. Same photo, same reading, every time. The models download once, about 24 MB, and stay in the browser.

Trying a look

Colour changes show on the photo, in the tab. A tuck or a belt shows on the chalk figure and by the break mark, never painted onto the photo.
